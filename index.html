The Biography of Late Pa. Francis Oluremi Ademakinwa

Pa. Francis Oluremi Ademakinwa was born in 1932 into the Ademakinwa family in Ile-Oluji, Ondo State.

He was a man of humble beginnings, resilience, and great industry. His life journey is a testimony of hard work, grace, and determination.

In 1946, at the young age of 14, he was brought to Lagos by one of his aunties. It was in Lagos that he began his life's journey from humble beginnings, building himself from scratch with honesty, hard work, perseverance, and absolute trust in God.

Family Life

Pa. Ademakinwa was a loving husband, father, grandfather and great-grandfather—a family man to the core who believed deeply in love, unity and legacy.

He was first married to the late Mrs. Racheal Morinola Ademakinwa, and later to Mrs. Iyabo Ademakinwa. His marriages were blessed with nine children:

  1. Late Mrs. Oladunni
  2. Mrs. Botu Ibironke
  3. Mrs. Apaleoha Tolulope
  4. Mrs. Koya Funmilayo
  5. Mr. Ademakinwa Segun
  6. Mrs. Okeme Folashade
  7. Late Master Ademakinwa Funsho
  8. Mrs. Iwalokun Ibiyemi
  9. Mrs. Amangele Oluwadamilola

Through them, he was blessed with many grandchildren and great-grandchildren.

Career and Business

Pa. Francis Oluremi Ademakinwa served meritoriously at the National Electric Power Authority (NEPA), where he worked diligently and with integrity until his retirement.

After retirement, he ventured into business, and the Lord blessed the works of his hands. He became a successful and respected businessman with a fleet of trailers, known across his circle for his integrity, generosity, godliness and kindness to all.

Faith and Legacy

Pa. Ademakinwa was more than a businessman; he was a patriarch, a man of prayer, a counsellor, and a devoted servant of God. He loved God passionately and served in His vineyard with joy and commitment. He was known for his wisdom, his calm spirit, and his willingness to help all who came to him.

He lived a fulfilled life of 94 years, leaving behind a legacy of faith, hard work, love for God, and love for family.

Transition to Glory

He passed on to glory peacefully on Wednesday, 10 June 2026. He is survived by his wife, children, grandchildren, great-grandchildren, family, and loved ones.
